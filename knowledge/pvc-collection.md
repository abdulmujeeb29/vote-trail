You must collect your Permanent Voter Card (PVC) in person. Nobody can collect it for you (no proxy). Only voters with a PVC can vote. Nationwide PVC collection for the 2027 elections began on 9 October 2026. Cards are usually ready one to three months after registration. INEC announces collection dates and places on its website and social media. Uncollected cards are kept by INEC, so you can still ask at your local INEC office.
