---
title: Fake registration websites
sources:
  - https://pmnewsnigeria.com/2026/07/09/cvr-inec-issues-alert-on-fake-online-registration-website/
---
INEC has warned about fake online registration websites. The only official portal for voter registration is cvr.inecnigeria.org. Registration is free. Never pay anyone to register you or to give you a voter's card, and never share your details on other sites.
