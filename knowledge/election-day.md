Go to your polling unit with your Permanent Voter Card (PVC). Officials accredit you with the BVAS device, which checks your fingerprint or face against the register. You then receive your ballot, vote in secret, and drop it in the box. After voting closes, ballots are counted in public at the polling unit. Party agents and observers can watch.
