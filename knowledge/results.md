After counting, the presiding officer records the results on Form EC8A, which is signed and posted at the polling unit. A photo of the form is uploaded from the BVAS to INEC's public Result Viewing portal (IReV, inecelectionresults.ng). The physical forms then go up the collation chain: ward, LGA, state, then national. If the upload fails, the signed Form EC8A remains the primary record for collation. Only INEC declares results.
