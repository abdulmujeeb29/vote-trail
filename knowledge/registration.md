Voter registration has two steps. First, pre-register online on INEC's Continuous Voter Registration portal at cvr.inecnigeria.org. Then go in person to an INEC state or LGA office, or the registration centre you chose, to complete your biometrics (fingerprints and photo). Registration is free. Check current registration dates on the INEC portal before you go.
