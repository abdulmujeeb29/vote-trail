---
title: Moving your registration to another polling unit
sources:
  - https://inecnigeria.org/?p=880
  - https://cvr.inecnigeria.org
---
If you moved away from where you registered, you can transfer your registration to a polling unit near you. You can do this online on the CVR portal (cvr.inecnigeria.org), which checks your identity with photos of your face against your existing record, or in person at an INEC office. You need your Voter Identification Number (VIN), which is on your voter's card.
