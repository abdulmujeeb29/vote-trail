KTECH FEST 3.0 HACKATHON CHALLENGE
==================================
"Every Vote, Every Voice"


THE PROBLEM
-----------
It's election morning in a small Nigerian town. Amaka has her voter's card, but
she's just found out her polling unit moved. When she finds the new one, the
queue is long and the officials are late. That evening the results are pasted
on the wall and people take photos. Days later, when the final numbers are
announced, many people wonder whether what they saw on that wall is what was
counted. Meanwhile, rumours about violence and vote-buying spread faster than
anyone can check them.

Amaka's story isn't unusual. A Nigerian election is a long chain: getting
voters informed and ready, identifying them, moving materials and staff,
counting, sending results onward, and settling disputes. One weak link is
enough to shake public trust in the whole outcome.


YOUR MISSION
------------
In 48 hours, pick one weak link in Nigeria's voting chain and build a solution
that makes it stronger.

Start by briefly showing how you see the bigger picture and where your piece
fits. Then go deep on that one piece. Your solution should work for real people
in real conditions:
  - voters with basic phones
  - voters who don't read English
  - places with poor network coverage
  - the polling officials, party agents and community leaders already involved
    on election day

It should add trust without adding friction.

Example areas you could tackle (you're not limited to these):
  - Helping voters find accurate information and fight misinformation
  - Making it easy to check results from the polling unit all the way to the
    final announcement
  - Reporting and verifying election-day incidents
  - Tracking election materials and staff
  - Making voting more accessible for elderly voters and people with
    disabilities


EXPECTED DELIVERABLES
---------------------
1. A one-page problem brief: the link you chose, why it matters, and who it
   affects.
2. A simple system sketch: a diagram showing how your solution fits into the
   wider election process.
3. A working prototype or clickable demo of your core feature.
4. A 3-minute live demo.
5. Submissions are made in two phases: the code to the GitHub repository, and
   also via the form:
   https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfjyF29W3iGvDvKeaeslv_XB3uok6LRWs6ncrps-bIEGoX8sw/viewform?usp=publish-editor


NOTE
----
These are the minimum, not the limit. If your team can go further, with extra
features, user testing, or a plan for how your solution could connect to the
rest of the system, you'll be rewarded for it. Judges will look at how clearly
you understand the problem, how original and practical your solution is, the
user experience, and how well your solution fits the realities of Nigerian
elections.


RULES
=====

1. Original Work
   1.1 All projects must be new work created during the hackathon.
   1.2 Teams may use open-source libraries, public APIs, pre-trained AI models
       and public datasets, but must credit them in their submission.
   1.3 Plagiarism, or submitting a project built before the event, leads to
       immediate disqualification.
   1.4 Teams may use AI coding assistants, but must declare them in the
       submission.

2. Submission Requirements
   Each team must submit the following through the submission link before the
   deadline:
   2.1 A public or shared GitHub repository with the source code.
   2.2 A demo video of no more than 3 minutes.
   2.3 A short write-up covering the problem, the solution, how AI is used, and
       the tools used.
   2.5 A live demo link, submitted via the form:
       https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfjyF29W3iGvDvKeaeslv_XB3uok6LRWs6ncrps-bIEGoX8sw/viewform?usp=publish-editor

3. Judging Criteria
   Projects will be scored on:
   3.1 Relevance to the theme ........................... 25%
   3.2 Innovation and creativity ........................ 20%
   3.3 Technical execution .............................. 25%
   3.4 Impact and scalability in the Nigerian context ... 20%
   3.5 Presentation and demo ............................ 10%
   3.6 A minimum score of 70% is required to make the top 5.

4. Selection and Second-Stage Screening
   4.1 Top teams will be shortlisted for second-stage screening and may be
       invited to present live at KTech Fest 3.0 in Abuja (3-5 December 2026).
   4.2 The judges' decision is final.

5. Intellectual Property
   5.1 Teams keep ownership of their projects.
   5.2 By taking part, teams allow the organisers to use project names,
       descriptions, screenshots and demo videos for promotion.

6. Disqualification
   The organisers may disqualify any team that breaks these rules, submits
   false information, or behaves in a way that harms the event or other
   participants.
