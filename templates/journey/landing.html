Trust

AI reads. Rules decide.

{{ APP_NAME }} never says who won. Only INEC declares results. We show what was counted, and what doesn't add up.

01

Two sources, or no mismatch

Two independent people must agree before a result is flagged. Rival party agents count the most.

02

Forwards aren't proof

The same message, image or device counts once, however many times it's shared.

03

Every quote is checked

If AI quotes a source and the words aren't really there, the quote is thrown out.
